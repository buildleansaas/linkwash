# LinkWash privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

LinkWash does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

- **Links you copy** are cleaned inside your browser by the extension's own code. They are never sent anywhere.
- **What it stores:** two values in Chrome's local extension storage on your device: whether LinkWash is turned on, and a count of links it has cleaned. Removing the extension deletes them.
- **Network:** the extension makes no network requests. The only links that leave the extension are the ones you click yourself in its popup (the source code on GitHub, the project page and the tip page).
- **Permissions:** it runs on every site because share buttons exist on every site. It reads what you copy only to check whether it is a single link, and only at the moment you copy.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/buildleansaas/linkwash/issues.
